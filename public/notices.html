How Radar Sees is an educational, interactive retelling of the physics and engineering described in three YouTube videos by the channel Noise In Space. All credit for the original analysis belongs to the creator — please watch the originals:

  • “Is Starlink A Secret Radar Constellation?”
  • “How The U.S. Will Track EVERY Vehicle from Space: SAR GMTI/AMTI”
  • “The Insane Engineering of Starlink V3”

Ported simulation engine — Noise In Space (MIT)

Data

  • Starlink orbital elements (TLEs) — courtesy of CelesTrak, Dr. T.S. Kelso. Fetched live and edge-cached for two hours to respect CelesTrak’s update cadence; a bundled snapshot serves as offline fallback.
  • Coastlines — Natural Earth (ne_110m_coastline), public domain.

Assets & fonts

  • Starlink satellite 3-D models — built procedurally for this site (no third-party assets). Dimensions follow SpaceX-published material: the Oct 2022 FCC filing, the “Brightness Mitigation Best Practices” paper (2022), and the Starlink V3 update.
