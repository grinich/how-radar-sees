We now have every piece. Part 1 gave us the radar link budget; Part 2, how an image is built; Part 3, how motion is tracked; Part 4, the specific radios Starlink flies. This final part puts them together and asks the title question: could Starlink's existing antennas — designed to sell internet — quietly work as a radar and signals-intelligence constellation? The answer is almost yes, and for one antenna, an emphatic yes.

Two problems stand between a communications satellite and a radar: it must hear a whisper while shouting, and its comms hardware must be coaxed into radar-like behaviour. We take the first problem first.
